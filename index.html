Editorial Principles

Baca:Tetapi is committed to developing responsible and transparent publishing practices.

  • Academic and intellectual integrity.
  • Respect for copyright and authorship.
  • Transparent use of artificial intelligence.
  • Evidence-based research and documentation.
  • Respect for cultural and linguistic diversity.
  • Protection of personal and research participant data.

Detailed editorial and peer-review policies will be established before journal submissions are opened.

About Baca:Tetapi

Baca:Tetapi is an independent digital publishing initiative based in Indonesia.

Baca:Tetapi dikembangkan sebagai ruang publikasi independen yang mempertemukan ilmu pengetahuan, sastra, pendidikan, teknologi dan warisan budaya.
